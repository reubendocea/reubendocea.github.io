Computer vision, robotics & applied AI

Throughout my career, I have pursued work that combines promising technology with the possibility of tangible impact. This has led me from founding a university society to 3D-print prosthetic devices for children in Glasgow, through agricultural robotics, to six years of research at the interface of computer vision and minimally invasive surgery. I now lead technology at Applied Computer Vision, developing and deploying computer vision systems for industrial settings. The role has broadened my work beyond engineering into client conversations and practical delivery. I am most interested in work that combines technical depth with a clear purpose, particularly where computer vision, robotics and AI can improve how people understand and act on the physical world.
